Purchase PaymentType update fix
================================

Replace app/billable_expense_cleanup.py with the corrected version and deploy.

What changed:
- Selected Purchase transactions are re-read directly by ID before updating.
- Purchase updates use the complete current transaction payload instead of a reconstructed sparse payload.
- PaymentType, AccountRef, and EntityRef are retained from the direct read, with the scan snapshot as fallback.
- The updater refuses to send a Purchase if PaymentType still cannot be determined.

After deployment, run a new scan and download a new CSV backup before retrying.
