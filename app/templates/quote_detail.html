SPH and markup rules

SPH is sales per labor hour: total quote revenue divided by estimated labor hours. Line markup % is calculated as line gross profit divided by line cost.

For Labor rows, the Qty / Labor Hrs cell is treated as hours. The app automatically copies that value into SPH Hrs when you save the row.
